Our Story

A Legacy Of Play Since 1983

Established 1983 Built on family trust

Founded in 1983, Franklite has grown with generations of young riders. Our journey began with a simple belief: childhood should be filled with safe, active and unforgettable play.

Today, we bring that same care to every ride-on toy, pedal bike and electric bike. Each product is selected for dependable construction, exciting design and the confidence parents look for every day.
