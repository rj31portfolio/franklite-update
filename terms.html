Terms & Conditions

Clear information about placing an order, making payment and receiving your Franklite products.

Delivery Timeline

3-4 Working Days

Orders are delivered within 3-4 working days after full payment has been successfully received.

Delivery timing may vary for remote locations, public holidays or circumstances outside our control.

1. Order Confirmation

An order is confirmed once we receive the required order details and full payment. We may contact you to confirm the product, delivery address and contact number before dispatch.

2. Payment

Full payment is required before an order is processed for dispatch. Please use the payment instructions shared by the Franklite team and retain your payment confirmation.

3. Delivery

After successful receipt of full payment, we aim to deliver your order within 3-4 working days. Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address to help us process your order without delay.

4. Product Information

Product images and colours are shown as accurately as possible. Actual product colours or details may vary slightly due to screen settings or product updates.

5. Order Changes

If you need to change an order or delivery detail, contact us as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible once an order has been processed or dispatched.

6. Need Help?

For questions about your order, payment or delivery, contact Franklite at +91 7428900713 or info@franklite.in.
